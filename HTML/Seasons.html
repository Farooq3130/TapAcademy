Spring

The world exhales, and colour returns

Frost loosens its grip. Cherry blossoms unfurl like whispered secrets across the hillside. The air fills with the scent of damp earth and new grass. Every branch reaches toward the light — a slow, defiant awakening.

MonthsMarch – May ToneRenewal · Tenderness ElementPetals on the wind
☀️

Summer

Light spills like honey across long days

The sun burns white at noon. Cicadas thrum in the heat haze, and the ocean gleams like molten glass. Evenings arrive reluctantly, painted in ribbons of vermilion and gold. This is the season that refuses to be modest — bold, shimmering, fully alive.

MonthsJune – August ToneRadiance · Vitality ElementSun-drenched gold
🍂

Autumn

The forest burnishes itself in farewell

Maple and oak catch fire from within. The air turns crisp and carries woodsmoke, cinnamon, the last sigh of apples. Leaves spiral down in slow motion — nature performing its most beautiful act of release. A season that teaches the grace of letting go.

MonthsSeptember – November ToneMelancholy · Warmth ElementEmbers in the trees
❄️

Winter

The earth turns inward, and silence deepens

Snow falls without hurry, erasing the sharp edges of the world. Each flake is a tiny star, momentary and perfect. Through bare branches, the moon looks closer. Inside, candles flicker — small defiant flames against the vast, dreaming dark. The stillness is not empty; it is full of waiting.

MonthsDecember – February ToneStillness · Wonder ElementStars falling to earth
🌿

Seasonalist

Ask me anything about the seasons

I am bound to the turning of the year. Ask me of spring rains, summer light, autumn leaves, or winter snow — I will answer. Ask me of other things, and I will hold my silence.
